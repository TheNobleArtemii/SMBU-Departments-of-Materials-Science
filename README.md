## 简介

本仓库收集并整理了深圳北理莫斯科大学（Shenzhen MSU-BIT University / МГУ-ППИ в Шэньчжэне）材料系（Departments of Materials Science, Faculty of Chemistry）学生学习过程中使用的各类资料，包括但不限于：课程讲义、复习大纲、习题集与参考解答、备考材料等。

**适用对象**：SMBU 材料系/化学系在读本科生，以及任何需要这些课程资料的同学。

> 注意：本仓库资料以俄语授课课程的配套材料为主，辅以中文复习资料。

## English

### About

This repository collects learning materials for students of the Departments of Materials Science, Faculty of Chemistry at Shenzhen MSU-BIT University (МГУ-ППИ в Шэньчжэне), including lecture notes, review outlines, exercise collections, and exam preparation materials.

**Audience**: undergraduate students of the Departments of Materials Science and Chemistry at SMBU.

Most materials are for courses taught in Russian, supplemented with Chinese review materials.
